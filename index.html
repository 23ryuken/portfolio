A little about me

I’m Eric, a full-stack developer who builds web apps from database to interface.

I trained at Moringa School and build mainly with the MERN stack, adding PostgreSQL when the data is relational. I lean towards the backend, where APIs, data models and business logic live.

I like owning a feature end to end, from schema to screen, and I believe the best work comes from curiosity, clear communication, and care for the details that make an experience feel complete.
